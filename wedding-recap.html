    Ideas for this page

    Log gifts the day you open them.Note who gave what and one detail you want to mention. Thank-yous get much easier to write.
    Split the writing.Assign each guest to whoever knows them best. The thank-you tab filters by writer.
    Aim for three months.That is the usual etiquette window after the wedding. The countdown above uses it.
    Write in batches of five.Move each card from Not started to Written to Mailed so neither of you double-writes.
    Check the registry completion discount.Most registries give 10 to 20% off remaining items for a few months after the date.
    Reuse the address list.Copy or download addresses for holiday cards and photo announcements.
